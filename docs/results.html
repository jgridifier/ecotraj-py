Results

Updated 2026-11-14 (ET). Milestones M0–M4 done; parity round 3–4 merged. Synthetic data only; scout-panel checks were run locally on data that is not in the repo.

Headline

Extension 2 passes its pre-registered test. The increment-level market/residual split with a re-projected sign-flip test held its false-positive rate at 0.052 (T = 40) and 0.053 (T = 62), inside the pre-set band [0.030, 0.070], and detected a planted orthogonal swap the size of the scout panel's 99.7% of the time at T = 62 (93.7% at T = 40). The old level-residualization procedure missed both criteria (false-positive rate 0.001, power 0.013).

3. Extension 2: pre-registered simulations (M3, M4)

The pre-registration was merged before any simulation (reviewed by a second agent; one dated addendum, also before the run). Design: 14-part compositions, 4 market covariates (one trending), market ≈ 35% of step energy; scenario (i) all directed motion is market-driven; scenario (ii) adds a part-1-vs-part-2 swap of 0.10 clr per step, orthogonal to every market loading. B = 199 sign flips, α = 0.05, R = 2000 (primary) / 1000 (secondary) replications, seed 2026111401.

Rejection rates at α = 0.05 with Clopper-Pearson 95% intervals; green = meets its pre-registered criterion, red = misses.

  • Verdict. P1 meets criterion 1 (size band at both T) and criterion 2 (power ≥ 0.80 at T = 62): Extension 2 passes. L misses both. Paired McNemar on (ii, T = 62): P1 rejected where L did not in 1,966 replications, the reverse in 0, so the pre-registered statement "P1 has more power than L" is allowed.
  • Secondary nulls. P1 stays in band under heavy tails and market-linked heteroskedasticity, and is conservative (0.000) when residual levels are stationary, which the pre-registration allows.
  • What the level procedure does. It is not anti-conservative; it is blind. Regressing levels on trending covariates soaks up the persistent residual drift, which is the scout run's p = 0.994 failure, now shown on known truth.
  • P1-naive (no re-projection; exactly what the scout ran) is slightly liberal at T = 40 (0.068), which is why the re-projected version is the one to use.
  • Caveat. Power is near 1 at T = 62, so the planted effect (taken from the scout panel) was easy; this run does not map power for smaller swaps. It does not show the scout panel's residual walk is real, and no forecasting metric was computed.
  • Provenance. The full run was repeated after a numba cache fix, with all caches cleared; every output file was byte-identical. Three replications were also recomputed independently with the slow reference code. See PROVENANCE.md and EXT2_RESULTS.md.
